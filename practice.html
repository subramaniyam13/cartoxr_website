Questions & answers

FAQ
How do GIS and XR work together?

GIS organises and analyses information about places. AR and VR can make it easier to inspect through navigation, immersive visualisation and interactive spatial tools. We select the format around the project question.

What geospatial work is represented?

The portfolio includes drone surveying, flood modelling, satellite-based prospectivity, accident mapping, and toll-plaza and road-network analysis. Each case describes its scope and source context.

Can you develop indoor navigation for Android and iOS?

The campus engagement includes Android and iOS applications. Positioning methods, hardware and site requirements are defined for each new project.

Do flood maps show an actual flood event?

The Meghalaya map is modelled inundation for a stated rainfall scenario, rather than observed flood extent. Inputs, assumptions and validation are part of interpreting the scenario.

Does satellite prospectivity confirm a mineral deposit?

Satellite analysis helps identify preliminary targets. Field investigation and further evidence are needed before making a deposit claim.

Do you offer workshops and academic content?

The portfolio includes workshops at IIST, Sardar Patel College of Engineering, CUTN and Bharati Vidyapeeth, Pune; fifteen SWAYAM cartography classes; and geography and geoinformatics course content.

What should a starting brief include?

Share the question to solve, the audience, available data or imagery, the intended platform and the target timeline. The contact page prepares an email draft for you to review and send.
