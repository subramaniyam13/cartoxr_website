About CartoXR

Where Geospatial Science Meets Immersive Technology

CartoXR pioneers the fusion of Geoinformatics and Extended Reality (XR). Founded by researchers and practitioners, we design and deliver rigorous geospatial solutions — from satellite-driven climate analysis and UAV flood mapping to AR/VR spatial visualization — for government, industry, and academia.

Based in Chennai with project experience across India and presented at international forums including ISPRS Perth 2024, we bring both research depth and delivery capability to every engagement.

What We Do

End-to-End Geospatial Services

From raw satellite imagery to immersive decision environments — we handle the full analytical pipeline.

🛰️
Satellite Image Analysis
Multi-temporal analysis using Sentinel-1/2, Landsat, MODIS. Land use change detection, vegetation indices (NDVI/EVI), land surface temperature, and spectral classification for climate monitoring.
🌊
Flood Risk & Hydrological Modelling
State-scale inundation mapping, flood extent delineation using SAR imagery, DEM-based drainage analysis, and disaster risk zoning for governments and insurers.
🌡️
Climate & Environmental Monitoring
Urban heat island mapping, air quality analysis via GEE, groundwater-LST interaction studies, and carbon stock assessment using optical and thermal remote sensing.
🚁
UAV Survey & Precision Mapping
High-resolution photogrammetry, 3D point cloud generation, orthomosaic production, and rapid deployment for emergency response and infrastructure inspection.
🥽
XR-based Spatial Visualization
AR/VR environments for geospatial decision-making — volumetric 3D model assessment inside VR, digital twin navigation, and immersive training simulations for field teams.
🎓
Capacity Building & Workshops
QGIS, Google Earth Engine, and XR training for government officials, urban planners, researchers, and academic institutions across India.
Our Work

Featured Projects

Real-world engagements across climate, disaster response, and immersive technology.

Climate · Flagship
State Government · Disaster Risk
State-Scale Flood Inundation Modelling — Meghalaya
Comprehensive flood risk assessment covering the entire state of Meghalaya using multi-source satellite data. Integrated Sentinel-1 SAR imagery for flood extent mapping, SRTM/ALOS DEM for terrain analysis, and hydrological modelling to delineate high-risk zones across river basins. Outputs included inundation depth maps, flood frequency zoning, and actionable risk layers for disaster management authorities.
Sentinel-1 SAR SRTM DEM Google Earth Engine Hydrological Modelling Flood Risk Zoning
UAV · Emergency Response
Bajaj Allianz · Insurance
UAV Flood Survey — 2023 Chennai Floods
Rapid deployment UAV mapping for post-flood damage assessment. Delivered high-resolution orthomosaics and 3D models enabling the client to quantify inundated areas and assess asset damage for insurance claims.
UAV Photogrammetry Orthomosaic Damage Assessment
Climate · Research
Research · Routledge India
Urban Air Pollution Mapping — Google Earth Engine
Mapped and analyzed spatial distribution of air pollution across Thiruvananthapuram city using Sentinel-5P and MODIS aerosol products on GEE. Identified seasonal hotspots and published findings in Routledge India's volume on disaster risk and resilient agriculture.
Sentinel-5P MODIS AOD Google Earth Engine Urban Climate
XR · Enterprise
LTI Mindtree · Enterprise
AR-based Indoor Navigation System
Developed an AR indoor navigation module using Unity as Library for seamless Android & iOS integration. Deployed for enterprise campus navigation with real-time spatial overlay.
Unity ARCore / ARKit Android · iOS
XR · Research · ISPRS

VR Tool for 3D Model Accuracy Assessment
Patented system for measuring and editing 3D spatial vector data inside a VR environment. Presented at ISPRS TC IV Symposium 2024 (TIF Grant Awardee) — enabling immersive quality assessment of photogrammetric reconstructions.
